# Sydney Tower Eye exterior reconstruction

This module, introduced in v0.1.3 and refined for v0.1.6, replaces the previously generic mapped tower and its office base with an independently built exterior. It is an architectural reconstruction from sources below, not a measured BIM or a playable elevator/observation-deck tour. No source photograph, raster logo, commercial 3D asset or plan image is packaged.

## Primary evidence inspected

- [Sydney Tower Eye operator facts](https://www.sydneytowereye.com.au/explore/explore/about-sydney-tower/) and [operator fact sheet](https://www.sydneytowereye.com.au/media/m3pniui5/ste-fact-sheet.pdf): 309m tip, 250m observation level, 268m SKYWALK, 6.7m shaft diameter, approximately 31m turret diameter, 56 cables and 420 windows. The present tower identity and dimensions take precedence over historic names or approximate third-party heights.
- [Official SKYWALK photograph](https://www.sydneytowereye.com.au/media/cdaa2zzr/look-at-skywalk.jpg), actually opened and viewed: broad gold windowed turret, narrower gold upper drum, red Westfield lettering, white plant enclosure, rails and projecting external platforms. [Official view page](https://www.sydneytowereye.com.au/explore/explore/the-view/) supplies skyline context.
- [BBR CONNAECT 2016, printed page10](https://www.bbrnetwork.com/fileadmin/userdaten/CONNAECT/CONNAECT_2016.pdf), downloaded only to `/tmp` for PDF rendering and visually inspected: reproduces its original 2009 Sydney Tower engineering article. It describes two groups of 28 straight cables forming a hyperboloid, a 37.2m roof anchorage circle, a 90m coupling point, a 140m neck connection and turret anchorage 184m above the roof. This establishes the cable arrangement rather than a guessed straight pole or decorative vertical lattice.
- [100 Market Street owner Link REIT](https://www.linkreit.com/en/business/properties/100-market-street/), actual owner-gallery exterior viewed: ten office storeys above the retail development, dark glazing and strong pale vertical fins. The mapped twelve-level tag is retained as twelve geometric damage bands, not described as twelve office floors.

## v0.1.6 reference review and visible changes

The following images were opened as browser image documents and actually inspected through browser screenshots on 12 September 2026. They were not inferred from search snippets, downloaded into this project, or used as game textures:

- [Operator aerial SKYWALK photo, 1200 x 628](https://www.sydneytowereye.com.au/media/cdaa2zzr/look-at-skywalk.jpg): the projecting rectangular glass platform, paired supports, diagonal braces, pale radial roof ribs, cleaning-track rings, glass roof-edge parapet, and white equipment enclosure with upright slots and rounded maintenance hoops.
- [Operator close-up SKYWALK photo, 1200 x 628](https://www.sydneytowereye.com.au/media/3dtnpwhn/skywalk-tour.jpg): narrow upper visitor gallery and lower maintenance gallery, gold support posts, multiple guardrail heights, large raised red letters, and their relationship to the gold drum and roof below.
- [Link REIT owner street-corner photo, 800 x 1200](https://channel823.linkreit.com/202003/img/content/link-reit-channel-823-mar-2020-first-overseas-acquisition-100-market-street-sydney-2.jpg): the glazed office facade above a contrasting retail base, vertical fins and shallow horizontal floor edges. [Current property page](https://www.linkreit.com/en/business/properties/100-market-street/) still describes ten office storeys.
- [Current operator homepage](https://www.sydneytowereye.com.au/) explicitly states **two glass platforms at 268m**. This is corroborated by the [NSW Government attraction entry](https://www.nsw.gov.au/visiting-and-exploring-nsw/locations-and-attractions/sydney-tower-eye).

The previous uniform circular SKYWALK treatment is replaced by two distinct rectangular, divided glass platforms, access aprons, roof-supported frames and diagonal bracing, and two narrow gallery levels. Guardrails have openings toward the upper platform access points. Roof detailing now includes radial ribs, a pair of circular track rails and separate glazed parapet panels. The white upper enclosure has flutes, upright slots, a maintenance gallery and rounded safety hoops. The turret underside has a stepped central collar and a radial rib pattern.

The turret window grid was also corrected: previous vertical bars passed through the middle of each window, visually doubling the number of bays. Mullions now sit between the 420 separately modeled panes. Red Westfield lettering is raised mesh geometry curved around the drum, generated from Godot's bundled outline font; it is an approximation of architectural lettering rather than a copied logo or external font asset. The office base distinguishes ten upper facade bands from two lower retail damage bands, with larger display glazing and cornices at the base.

## Geography and height limits

The module uses the polygon centroid of OSM way197801072 from the checked-in source snapshot: latitude **-33.8704954659**, longitude **151.2089471132**. The existing map conversion produces **(-143.48673656, 4.5, 1168.3552663)** metres. The base follows the exact point sequence of way197801073. Replaced source ways are **197801072, 197801073, 197801074 and 273960049**; the rest of Westfield is not removed. The source snapshot is `source/map-data/city.json` and is ODbL map data, not a cadastral survey.

The street arrival **(-168.4, 4.5, 1190.8)** is on the public Market Street side, outside the building footprint. It is separate from the map/building centre and does not claim to be the actual Level5 ticket desk. The module does not add an operating lift or navigate players onto a closed tower floor.

The 309m top, shaft/turret diameters, cable count, window count, two glass platforms and 268m SKYWALK elevation are source dimensions or counts. The **55m base roof height is estimated** by fitting the source's roof-relative cable arrangement to a roughly239m underside anchorage; it is not an independently measured roof elevation. Turret band heights, cladding depth, cable angular offsets and thicknesses, plant profile, lettering font and individual rail/panel geometry are reconstructed approximations. In v0.1.6 the approximately 5.6m by 5.2m glass-platform envelope, platform bearings and assumed opposite arrangement, 1.26m gallery width, 265.8m lower maintenance deck, guard heights, 70 roof ribs, 42 plant flutes/slots and 14 safety hoops are visual modeling estimates, not surveyed dimensions or verified component counts. The two lower retail damage bands represent visible frontage zoning only; they do not claim to reproduce the five actual shopping levels or the shopping-centre interior. The cylindrical turret still uses four evenly distributed window bands, so its exact floor elevations and pane schedule remain approximations. The world still uses its existing flat4.5m terrain datum, which is not an AHD height or an exact reproduction of street grades.

## Geometry and verification

The model has 56 geometric cable paths with coupling/neck details, a segmented shaft, stepped turret underside, four bands totalling 420 physical window panels, a gold drum with two exterior gallery levels and two framed glass platforms, detailed white upper plant room, tapering communications spire and navigation beacon. The office appearance uses mapped perimeter geometry with separate facade fins, retail display bays and floor-edge details. All 33 original structure/damage IDs are retained. Major body sections have damage collisions and grouped details; cables, rails, lettering and elevated platforms remain attached visual geometry rather than individually simulated ropes or an accessible visitor route.

The focused command is:

```sh
tools/runtime/godot --headless --path game --script ../source/sydney_tower_landmark_test.gd
```

The v0.1.6 local run passes **30/30** checks with **33** structure records and **121,772** triangles (below the existing 130,000 limit; v0.1.3 had 103,264). Tests inspect actual platform/window geometry, the 268m deck elevation, roof-to-deck support extent, mesh winding, triangle degeneracy, exact solid/collision correspondence, original damage IDs, pedestrian arrival clearance and high-level collision rays. The damage test verifies that removing the drum removes exactly its platform glazing from the rendered mesh batches, and repair restores those vertices and collision.

`capture_views()` exposes three `[name, global_camera_position, global_look_at]` entries for full tower, SKYWALK close-up and street podium. The coordinating task also ran the local fixture with `--capture`: all 30 checks passed and five direct native GPU images were saved to `reports/sydney-tower-v016/`; the turret and street views were visually inspected. This is an isolated fixture. Complete-city viewpoints and final packaged-App evidence belong in `TESTING.md`. Earlier v0.1.3 captures remain historical comparison material under `reports/sydney-tower-v013/` and do not verify this revision.
