# Manly exterior reconstruction

Reviewed 10 September 2026. All game geometry is original procedural work. Photographs were viewed in a browser for proportions and architectural features; no photographic pixels or third-party 3D assets are shipped.

## Map evidence

- `source/map-data/manly.json`: OpenStreetMap / Overpass source, base timestamp 6 May 2026. Manly Ferry Terminal is way **7981564**; The Corso pedestrian area is **223785178**, with route **546705240**. Its western end also contains actual vehicle streets: this model does not pave over that traffic loop.
- `source/map-data/manly_landmark_detail.json`: targeted additional Overpass query from `https://overpass-api.de/api/interpreter`, base timestamp **10 September 2026 10:41:50 UTC**. Hotel Steyne is multipolygon **relation 7889573**, outer way **223786812**, inner courtyard way **552008767**. The true courtyard was retained; an offline constrained triangulation covers 1,560.175 m² of the building ring without filling the courtyard.
- `source/map-data/trees.json` and the compiled `game/assets/city_map.json`: mapped tree points. Species `Araucaria heterophylla`, genus Araucaria, Norfolk pine names and needle-leaved categories receive a branch-whorl pine model. Exact mapped positions are retained. Unclassified tree points inside The Corso are rendered as palms based on the inspected streetscape photograph; that species choice is an inference. Other Manly trees use the game's ordinary canopy model.
- Mapped bus platform nodes: Stand A **6388477486**, Stand B **6388478985**, Stand C **497623847**. Signs identify the physical stops without inventing live services, schedules or a working transit simulation.

All coordinates use the existing origin latitude −33.86, longitude 151.2105 and the existing planar metres-per-degree scale. OpenStreetMap geometry is © OpenStreetMap contributors, ODbL; the distributed map attribution and source data remain separate from the original model geometry.

## Scope and limits

The three detailed groups are Manly Wharf, The Corso and Hotel Steyne. The surrounding terrain, roads and ordinary building footprints come from the separate city map system. Wharf superstructure height (13.2m clock tower), Hotel Steyne height (11.4m to raised parapet), window rhythm, columns, awnings and timber seat placement are photo-based estimates, not survey measurements. Tree heights are illustrative. The platform and city use the game's 4.5m flat ground datum. Interiors, ferry services, individual shop stock and a fully surveyed city elevation model are not implemented by this module.

The Corso centreline remains physically clear. Its pavement is 9.5cm above the terrain datum to sit above the city's road ribbon without flickering. Wharf entrances are open columns, not a single solid building box. Hotel Steyne's courtyard remains a real physical opening. Building components use the game's existing persistent damage IDs; street furniture and foliage use spatial instancing.

## Wharf–Corso–Steyne public-route refinement — 12 September 2026

This pass improves **one continuous Manly core route**, including the public front concourse inside the wharf. It does not establish complete Manly coverage or one-to-one current as-built accuracy. The exact geographic origin, existing wharf/hotel shell outlines, hotel courtyard and old component IDs remain. Other streets and shop exteriors still depend on the ordinary city model.

### Street layout and continuity

Previously `_corso` placed regularly spaced ring seats independently from `_beach_pines`, which already placed ring seats at mapped Corso palms. This produced an unsupported second population of empty tree rings. The regular inferred seat row has been removed; the mapped palms and their existing photo-informed rings remain. The original pedestrian polygon and vehicle turnaround remain unchanged.

The continuous public route follows the wharf's open north entrance, the mapped approach links **135133937 / 7981630**, the existing west Corso street connection, and pedestrian route **546705240**, ending by Hotel Steyne. The game still uses its flat 4.5m datum (4.595m on the raised Corso paving and 4.540m on the corrected wharf platform); slope differences, exact kerb ramps and the real waterfront elevation are not surveyed. Navigation reads the four canonical records from `game/assets/landmark_geography.json`: `manly_wharf`, `manly_corso`, `hotel_steyne` and `manly_beach`. The older `the_corso` spelling remains an alias of `manly_corso`, not a second map destination. Actual mapped centres and all arrival X/Z coordinates remain unchanged. The wharf destination remains the existing northern public entrance approach; only Corso and Hotel Steyne arrival Y was corrected from 4.5m to the physical paving top at 4.595m. The shared registry and module now read the same records.

### Corso palm crown correction after exported-App image review

The exported App's `manly_corso_seating.png` exposed a sparse, almost coplanar umbrella shape with comb-like leaf fragments. The Corso reference photograph above was opened and inspected again: visible palms have dense split fans and hanging older brown leaves. This supports that general crown structure, but does **not** independently establish each mapped tree's exact species, pruning state or dimensions.

Only `_palm` crown geometry and its new helpers changed. The source tree points, trunk geometry and its **estimated 11m** height, ring seats, other tree categories, buildings, navigation and all collisions are unchanged. Each crown now has **24 green fans and 10 hanging old fans**, each divided into **13 individual curved lobes**. Leaf stalks are curved closed tubes; each lobe has a folded diamond section, a raised ridge **18–43mm** above its centerline and an underside **18mm** below it. These thicknesses are a deliberate readable game representation, not botanical measurements. The overlapping leaves occupy several heights instead of forming one flat sheet. No new photographic texture is used.

The crown uses two cached meshes shared between trees: **15,648 green triangles + 6,520 old-leaf triangles = 22,168 triangles per crown**. Relative to the tree's existing root position, the measured mesh bounds are **X −2.975…3.135m, Y 8.525…12.885m, Z −3.015…3.091m**. These crown triangles are separate from the unchanged **18,284 structural triangles / 261 persistent components**.

The dedicated `-- --palm-only` mode checks 45 assertions: closed leaf/stalk surfaces, no degenerate triangles, normalized correctly wound normals, actual folded cross-sections, drooping old leaves, bounded crown geometry and exact mapped instance positions. The post-crown combined fixture at that stage ran those 45 together with all previous 47 checks, including both continuous production-Player routes, and passed **92/92**, exit 0, without errors or warnings. `reports/manly-palm/checks.json` contains the dedicated mesh count/bounds; `reports/manly-palm/combined-checks.json` binds the complete final source hashes. The original sparse-crown App images remain baseline evidence; corrected-crown native acceptance is a separate parent-run review and was not performed by this source-only check.


### Wharf platform separation and old-save safety

The parent-run native local preview exposed large irregular green patches inside the wharf public hall. A triangle probe at four hall positions found **both** the visible mapped `OSM_Land_0` surface and `manly/wharf/platform` top at exactly **Y=4.500m**. This confirmed actual coplanar geometry rather than assuming the patches were normal shadows. The pre-fix five images, 92-check report and probe log remain in `reports/manly-floor/before/`.

Only the existing platform top is raised by **0.040m** to **Y=4.540m**. Its mapped X/Z outline, original −1m local bottom, component ID and footprint remain unchanged; the same closed mesh supplies visible geometry and collision. The two interior foot positions of the continuous walk routes now use 4.540m. The canonical wharf navigation arrival is outside the platform on ordinary ground, so its coordinates remain unchanged. This four-centimetre offset is an authored game surface separation, not a claim of surveyed ferry-terminal elevation.

The complete Manly fixture now passes **109/109 checks**, exit 0, with a clean log. At each of four open-floor samples, the visible mesh and actual physics ray agree on 4.540m while the underlying terrain remains 4.500m. New valid standing positions are preserved. Temporary old ground-height poses are raised at the same X/Z to 4.580m, the floor plus the migration helper's existing four-centimetre clearance. The exact wharf-platform ID is accepted by the shared player recovery support filter; actual overlapping benches are still rejected, and destroyed platform support is not accepted. No real save files are read or rewritten.
