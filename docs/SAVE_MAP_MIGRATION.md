# Map revision migration

The current v0.1.3 city records `map_revision: 3`. New writes use save format 5 and the reader accepts formats 1–4. Saves older than map revision 3 run the map relocation pass; a current revision save is restored without repeated relocation. Vehicle model revision 3 is recorded independently. Revision 2 below describes the retained bridge compatibility rules.

`map_migration.gd` checks the full stored vehicle envelope and the player's capsule-sized bounds against live mapped building solids. Polygon intersection and courtyard subtraction preserve concave outlines and holes. Building-part base heights preserve public ground under elevated structures. Destroyed storey components are excluded. Custom city, bank, Quay, Manly, metro and frontage structures use their real collision shapes; closed trimesh containment catches an object entirely inside a shell. Open meshes are not automatically considered solid interiors.

If displacement is necessary, each existing vehicle searches near its own saved location using the ordinary full-footprint spawn planner. Its ID, health, fuel, ownership and other persistent fields survive. Movement velocities are reset at a safe replacement pose. The originally occupied copy and the last summoned copy's waypoint are restored after migration. The player receives a count of safe map/parking adjustments. Loading itself does not rewrite the source file; a subsequent normal save records the revisions.

Replacing the former straight north bridge approach restores mapped buildings in its old corridor. Revision 2 rechecks those live building solids. An additional narrowly bounded compatibility check recognises the old ramp's exact endpoints, width and support height; when that retired surface no longer supports a saved road vehicle or on-foot player, it finds a safe replacement. The new curved approach follows the actual mapped Bradfield Highway; only its grade remains estimated. The revised southern springing also raises the south-ramp plane at former parking positions (about 1.56m at the old midpoint and 2.35m at three-quarter distance). A second bounded check uses the exact v0.1.1 south endpoints, width and contact height. It recovers only stationary road vehicles and on-foot players that lack an upward-facing surviving support. When the new south surface is present and clear, it retains X/Z near the saved point and aligns the vehicle with the actual ramp normal; otherwise it uses the existing safe-placement search. A downward-facing bridge underside is not accepted as a road, and a destroyed ramp does not count as support. Moving/rotating/tilted vehicles, aircraft crossing the old plane, moving pedestrians and normal ground positions below the bridge are preserved.

`vehicle_model_migration.gd` checks stationary old car, motorcycle, yacht and airliner copies against the replacement model envelope. Ground support is sampled within 0.75m below the expected old wheel contact before planning any adjustment. It therefore preserves safe bridge-underpass parking and unsupported aircraft in flight, instead of finding a higher bridge deck or pulling an aircraft down. Overlapping old yacht berths may be relocated for the new larger hulls. New revision copies are not automatically repositioned on each load.

The same custom-solid containment check is used by vehicle summoning so that an airborne waiting wing cannot be created wholly inside a custom tower. The checks are cached from the final world geometry and still consult live destruction state.

## Verification

Run `tools/runtime/godot --headless --path game --script ../source/map_migration_test.gd` from the repository root. This uses the real main load/save methods, vehicle models, physics server, imported-building extrusion code and custom prism construction inside a small isolated scene. It never calls the save-slot menu or reads non-QA world IDs. Fixture files have a unique `qa_map_migration_` prefix and are retained, including their recovery backups. All 52 checks pass on Jolt, including v0.1.3 authored solids and queued-motion regressions.

`source/vehicle_model_migration_test.gd` passes **21 focused Jolt checks** with zero script errors (`reports/model-migration-v012.log`). In addition to the original eight north-ramp, bridge-underpass, stationary/low-flight and identity checks, an actual sloped south-support collider exercises parked vehicles at 25%, 50%, 75% and 90% of the old ramp, pedestrians at 50% and 75%, preserved health/fuel/freeze/heading, and unchanged moving vehicles, low flight, moving pedestrians and normal bridge-underpass positions. These are local physics fixtures, so they do not emit the complete-city READY marker. `source/experience_flow_test.gd` verifies that two formerly overlapping compact yacht berths separate safely after an actual load while preserving damage, fuel, occupied aircraft and the original save bytes.

The regression fixtures cover a very tall hollow collider, a courtyard, a courtyard with a masonry notch, raised building parts, a closed custom tower, a solid metro column, a destroyed imported building, nearby full-width aircraft relocation, on-foot recovery, remote fleet copies, occupied vehicle restoration, unchanged safe copies, revision gating and source-file preservation. Baseline evidence is kept in `reports/map-migration-baseline.log` and `reports/map-migration-spawn-baseline.log`; final assertions are in `reports/map-migration.json`.

Geometry and facade reconstruction limitations are documented in the individual landmark reference files. Migration prevents occupied solid volumes; it does not turn unmapped interiors into playable rooms.


## v0.1.3 updates

Map revision 3 rechecks saved actors against the expanded W / ICC, Sydney Tower and quay geometry. It uses the existing damage-aware conflict checks; safe saved positions are retained. Vehicle-model revision 3 also checks parked legacy helicopters against the larger rotor/airframe envelope while retaining their original support surface. Moving and unsupported airborne helicopters are preserved. The focused migration fixture now includes three helicopter assertions (24 total); final release evidence is linked in [TESTING.md](TESTING.md).

New gliders queue their initial speed inside the first physical integration step. A save made before that step records the queued velocity; loading a flying vehicle restores its actual velocity without injecting new launch energy. The hoverboard also retains its requested lift offset. Format 5 prevents the released v0.1.2 reader, whose maximum is 4, from loading a new save and silently discarding an unrecognised hoverboard on its next save. Merely loading an old file does not overwrite it; the next successful save writes format 5 and preserves the previous file as its recovery backup.

Safety relocation clears queued motion as well as current linear/angular velocity. An immediate save and the next physics integration therefore both remain stopped. A safe flying aircraft is not relocated and retains its saved motion. Sydney Tower, Circular Quay and Darling Square detail use the same damage-aware solid containment checks as the older landmarks; destroyed openings and public entrances remain usable.

`tools/test_save.gd` passes 20 isolated save checks. The historical reader fixture at `source/fixtures/save_store_v012.gd` is byte-identical to commit `d6ddb4d` and the final v0.1.2 build manifest (SHA-256 `da9a206bdd26938774f91353aafb7db701ca7658b7f45498a31d590c0609918d`). The test executes that actual reader against a valid v4 control and the new v5 file: it accepts v4, rejects v5 and leaves both save and backup bytes untouched. The new reader also accepts each format 1–4 without rewriting its source; upgrading a v4 world preserves the original car and two newly added independent hoverboards, their pose, movement, damage, lift offset and occupied/target IDs. All generated `qa_store_` fixtures and backups are retained; no player world is read or deleted. Evidence is recorded in `reports/save-v013-format5.json` and its corresponding log.
