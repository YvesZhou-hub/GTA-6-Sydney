# v0.1.7 evidence

Seven final native runs are bound by executable and PCK hashes to the SHA-verified ZIP in build.json. Reports, fresh logs and selected PNGs are checked before any output is replaced. Local machine paths in text are normalized; original byte hashes are retained in evidence-index.json. Screenshots are copied without editing.

Source fixtures remain separate from final App checks. Only their declared dependencies are attributed to the current build. Obsolete source reports, candidate captures and local calibration renders are not silently substituted. This evidence does not establish complete 1:1 geography, photogrammetry, FPS or human hardware-input coverage.
