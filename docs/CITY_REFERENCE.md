# Sydney named building references

Research and reconstruction: 10 September 2026. Implementation: `game/scripts/city_landmarks.gd`. These four buildings are native, metre-scale procedural meshes with structural collision and removable damage components. They are **photo-based exterior reconstructions**, not laser scans or a claim of a complete 1:1 Sydney. No reference photograph, third-party texture, logo or interior plan is redistributed.

## Location and evidence quality

The existing world projection is retained: `X=(longitude−151.2105)×92400`, `Z=(−33.86−latitude)×111320`; +X is east and +Z south. Ground is the game's 4.5m datum, not a site-specific AHD survey. Polygon centroids come from the OSM geometry, not street-address geocoding. All footprint vertices are converted to local metres and retained to 0.00001m numerical precision; that precision does **not** imply OSM has survey accuracy.

## International Tower One / HSBC

The current [HSBC Australia company page](https://www.about.hsbc.com.au/) identifies its headquarters in Barangaroo; [HSBC's contact directory](https://www.hsbc.com/contact-us) gives One International Towers Sydney, 100 Barangaroo Avenue. This is the headquarters' host building, not an invented standalone “HSBC tower”.

[RSHP's International Towers project](https://rshp.com/projects/office/international-towers-sydney/) and [completion account](https://rshp.com/news/archive/rshp-completes-international-towers-at-barangaroo-sydney/) describe the architect's tower family, cores, floorplates and external environmental treatment. [CTBUH's Tower One entry](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/barangaroo-southinternational-towers/13280) supplies the 217m architectural height. Its historical address field is not preferred over the tenant's current official address.

The [real Tower One exterior photograph on Wikimedia Commons](https://upload.wikimedia.org/wikipedia/commons/2/24/International_Towers_Sydney_tower_1.jpg) was opened and visually inspected in the browser. Its rounded glass body, narrow red sunshade blades, dense vertical mullions, horizontal metal floor edges, dark intermediate village bands and northern service-core articulation inform the model. The exact concave/rounded OSM tower outline is preserved instead of replacing it with a cylinder or box. Repeated metal details are merged into each of the 49 removable floor components.

## Bank of China, 140 Sussex Street

The bank's [official 2019 relocation notice](https://www.bankofchina.com/au/en/bocinfo/bi1/201907/t20190710_15913196.html) moves its Australian head office to Ground Floor, 140 Sussex Street from 12 August 2019. [The current official Australian company page](https://www.bankofchina.com/au/en/aboutus/) confirms this address. The older 39–41 York Street headquarters and the unrelated Hong Kong Bank of China Tower must not be substituted.

[TTW's Bank of China project](https://www.ttwengineers.com/projects/bank-of-china) describes refurbishment of the existing 1988 building's 15 levels, including rooftop garden/pergolas and service work. The project page was available in search during research; direct navigation subsequently redirected to its project index. [Rickard Engineering's facade account](https://rickardengineering.com/the-use-of-very-small-undercut-concealed-anchors-on-grc-fc-building-facades/) identifies tiled GRC facade panels at 140 Sussex Street. [Climate Active's building record](https://www.climateactive.org.au/buy-climate-active/certified-members/140-sussex-street-sydney) independently confirms the building's Bank of China association.

The building photo in [AFR's 2016 purchase report](https://www.afr.com/property/bank-of-china-buys-new-hq-for-130m-20160905-gr9c5a) was opened and visually inspected. It shows pale tiled piers/spandrels, dark rectangular glazing, a shallow outward central street bay and a glazed ground level. This photograph predates the 2019 refurbishment, so the reconstruction does not assert that entrance signs or every current facade detail match. The game adds neither an old ING sign nor a fabricated bank logo. The roof additions express documented use, with approximate geometry rather than an invented surveyed plan.

## The Ribbon / W Sydney

[Marriott's W Sydney overview](https://www.marriott.com/en-us/hotels/sydwh-w-sydney/overview/) supplies the address, hotel identity and rooftop facilities. The [Marriott-issued opening announcement](https://www.prnewswire.com/news-releases/w-hotels-unveils-architectural-icon-in-australias-waterfront-metropolis-with-the-opening-of-w-sydney-301955782.html) identifies the opening and curving building design. [CTBUH's Ribbon entry](https://www.skyscrapercenter.com/building/the-ribbon-hotel-and-residences/14434) gives the 90m height and 25 storeys.

The completed exterior on [Karabiner's project page](https://karabiner.com.au/projects/the-ribbon), specifically [this photograph](https://www.karabiner.com.au/projects/the-ribbon/a889c8b37021bc4f395a9f40d104d073.jpeg), was opened and visually inspected. It shows the long rising, rounded silhouette, blue-gray glass grid and warm metal perimeter. The model has a 29-degree plan orientation; its long axis runs southwest–northeast and its taller rounded end is northeast. Cubic curves generate the upper outline, and closed geometry is clipped into 25 floor bands without visible gold lines at the damage seams. The mapped podium follows the actual irregular footprint. Surrounding Western Distributor flyovers belong to the road/elevation model and are not invented as part of the hotel. No W/IMAX sign or restaurant interior is copied or fabricated.

## The Exchange / Haidilao Darling Square

The [architect's completed project page](https://kkaa.co.jp/en/project/the-exchange/) records 2019 completion, 6,680m² and 7F, shifted floor plates and overlapping curved Accoya timber strips. Its [completed-building photo by Martin Mischkulnig](https://kkaa.co.jp/img/2019/12/52794ec0791daa69174ff46b332832ef.jpg) was opened and visually inspected: pale irregular horizontal timber, circular glass drums, broad terrace overhangs and a visually open ground level. [WoodSolutions' project record](https://www.woodsolutions.com.au/case-studies/exchange) confirms the address, construction material and floorplate/terrace arrangement. [The City of Sydney's library announcement](https://news.cityofsydney.nsw.gov.au/articles/new-library-at-darling-square-to-take-flight-in-2019) confirms two library floors and the completed timber-screen concept.

The [NSW planning portal BCA assessment](https://majorprojects.planningportal.nsw.gov.au/prweb/PRRestService/mp/01/getContent?AttachRef=SSD-7021%2120190227T091632.991+GMT) states seven storeys and effective height 25.55m, based on top occupied floor RL28.00 minus ground RL2.45. This is not a certified roof height. The game's approximate 30m roof envelope allows the upper restaurant volume above that occupied floor. The [operator's store directory](https://www.superhiinternational.com/serve/storesearch) identifies Haidilao at Level 5, The Exchange, 1 Little Pier Street; the selected branch remains the explicit working assumption until the user specifies another branch.

The model uses seven independently removable offset circular bodies with broad pale slab edges, dense irregular 3D timber strips and fine screen supports. Offsets, timber spacing and glazing division follow the observed appearance, not an as-built shop drawing. Timber is batched per floor and material, not thousands of scene nodes. Timber screens and other small facade attachments are decorative geometry; the closed structural body supplies collision, and both disappear together on damage. No retail interior is implied.

## Data rights and checks

OSM footprint data: © OpenStreetMap contributors, [ODbL attribution and licence](https://www.openstreetmap.org/copyright). Source snapshot is in `source/map-data/city.json`. Its mapped positions and tags are combined with primary architectural/occupier references; erroneous or obsolete tags are documented above rather than silently treated as current truth.

Run `./tools/runtime/godot --headless --path game --script ../source/city_landmark_test.gd`. Add `-- --visual` without `--headless` for native screenshots in `/tmp/harbourlife-city-review/`. The check covers coordinate conversion, correct OSM replacement IDs, material/geometry bounds, outward triangle winding, exact structural mesh/collider correspondence, actual physics ray hits at each geographic position, damage and restoration, and a bounded combined triangle count. Native exterior views are inspected separately because correct bounds alone do not establish a recognizable silhouette.
