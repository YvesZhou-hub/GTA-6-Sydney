The asset was obtained directly from Poly Haven. The user-selected [GTA_SZ reference project](https://github.com/linranff/GTA_SZ) uses this sky too; its code, models and processed assets were not copied. Harbourlife's sky shader and engine integration are original Godot code.

This is a lighting asset, not photographic evidence of Sydney, local weather or a surveyed sun angle. It provides the visible daytime sky and matching material reflection/ambient environment. The static calibration preset aligns its light with the source's brightest pixel. During the city time cycle, the shader suppresses the photograph's fixed solar core and uses the independently calculated summer solar direction for the visible solar disc and direct light. See [the additional day/night-cycle assets](DAYLIGHT_CYCLE.md); the photographic cloud and lighting treatment remains artistic rather than a local weather simulation.
