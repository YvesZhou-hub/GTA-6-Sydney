# Asset and dependency provenance

- Engine: Godot 4.7.2 stable, official `ed1daf0bf`, MIT. Exact bundled license and third-party copyright notices are adjacent. The runtime and macOS template were reused from the already installed official toolchain; no unrelated game assets/code were copied. A local ad-hoc signature was applied to the standalone CLI and generated app. No Apple Developer ID or notarization is claimed.
- Game models: original deterministic source geometry in `game/scripts/harbor_world.gd`, `bridge_landmark.gd`, `opera_landmark.gd`, `airport_world.gd`, `vehicle_factory.gd`, `harbor_player.gd`, `harbor_npc.gd`, `harbor_cargo.gd`. Meshes, façade elements, vegetation and collisions are generated at startup from these maintainable sources; the geographic database and ordinary pitched-roof surfaces are compiled offline. No external aircraft/vehicle models, branding or livery packs are included.
- Textures/shaders: original generated paving/stone textures, spherical roof tile shader, window façade shader and water shader. No downloaded satellite/aerial photographs, streamed city meshes, Google map tiles or photogrammetry are redistributed.
- Landmark photographic references: official Sydney Opera House spherical design and tile documentation, Transport for NSW heritage dimensions, and real ground/aerial photographs were consulted for original geometry. Reference links and the modelling approximations are recorded in `../docs/BRIDGE_REFERENCE.md` and `../docs/OPERA_REFERENCE.md`. Reference images are not redistributed as game assets.
- Audio: original deterministic synthesized waveforms for environment, footsteps, water, engines, rotor and impacts. No music library or external recordings.
- Font: runtime `SystemFont` requests the user's installed Avenir Next / PingFang / Arial, with engine fallback. No Apple/Microsoft font file is copied or redistributed. Godot fallback font notices are in its copyright file.
- Geography: see `GEOGRAPHY.md`, `../reports/GEOGRAPHY.md`, `../reports/AIRPORT.md` and included source records. OpenStreetMap-derived data remains under ODbL and is attributed in the game credits. OurAirports runway records are published as public domain. Official Sydney Airport lengths were used to reconcile rounded endpoint coordinates.
- Expanded city assets: `city_map.gd`, `city_landmarks.gd`, `bank_landmarks.gd`, `quay_landmarks.gd`, `cyber_landmarks.gd`, `darling_square_frontages.gd`, `manly_landmarks.gd`, `metro_entrances.gd` and `icc_landmarks.gd` contain the authored reconstruction. Company/property/architect sources and directly inspected photographs are listed in the individual `../docs/*REFERENCE.md` files. Ordinary OSM facades remain inferred; see [city coverage and precision](../docs/CITY_DATA.md). Source photographs and trademark logo artwork are not included as textures.
- Ordinary pitched roofs: `tools/city_roofs.py` derives 524 profiles from explicit OSM `roof:shape` tags, with 280 hipped, 212 gabled, 29 skillion and 3 pyramidal roofs. Only 7 carry `roof:height`; the other 517 roof rises are estimated from footprint width and capped against building height. Tagged dimensions have not been independently surveyed. Ridge alignment uses the footprint's minimum rotated bounding rectangle and `roof:orientation`; `roof:direction` is not currently consumed. These are coloured geometric surfaces, not surveyed roof models, individual tiles or photographic textures. Their map-derived geometry retains the source database's ODbL provenance.
- Cyber office buildings: original reconstructions of CyberCX's NSW office building at Level 23, 2 Market Street, and Cloudflare's Sydney office building at 388 George Street. CyberCX's Australian headquarters is in Melbourne; neither Sydney model is labelled a headquarters. The six ordinary OSM volumes replaced are ways `1521293802`, `335699164`, `335699165`, `1521293801`, `386563854` and `386563852`; neighbouring Hooker House is retained. Official company addresses, property/architect photographs and published architectural heights are distinguished from estimated facade subdivisions in [the reference record](../docs/CYBER_REFERENCE.md). No private office interiors or invented large company facade logos are included.
- ICC venues: `game/scripts/icc_landmarks.gd` is integrated in the main-world loader, using the attributed OSM-derived footprints in `game/assets/icc_geometry.json` for ICC Sydney's Convention and Exhibition Centres and the adjacent TikTok Entertainment Centre performance venue. This is not a TikTok corporate office model. Original geometry includes the documented public foyer, mezzanine, lower exhibition halls and simplified theatre areas. Public plans, photographs, estimated dimensions and inaccessible areas are distinguished in [the ICC reference record](../docs/ICC_REFERENCE.md). Map-derived footprints remain under ODbL; no reference-photo pixels or PDF pages are distributed as game assets. Source integration and regression checks do not establish final release publication.
- Road surfaces: `city_map.gd` constructs continuous ribbons and round joins from the same OSM centrelines. Pedestrian paving is geometrically trimmed where it overlaps a carriageway, with a 2mm numerical clipping allowance; neither road centrelines nor road elevations are shifted. Excavation polygons also subtract from new join surfaces. The 17 geometric checks in `source/road_join_test.gd` cover the original 14 seam/opening checks plus three material-overlap checks; [the report](../docs/evidence/road-joins.json) records 316 repaired sample points and zero tested street-material/metro-opening overlap area. These checks establish generated geometry consistency, not independently surveyed road precision or completion of native visual review.
- Offline map tooling: Shapely 2.1.2 (BSD 3-Clause) and its GEOS dependency are used to triangulate/clip the attributed source database. The development virtual environment is excluded from source and player archives. The generated database, conversion script, provenance and validation fixtures are included; Shapely is not needed to play or export the already compiled map.
- Video development tool: imageio-ffmpeg/FFmpeg 7.1 obtained from PyPI for encoding captured game frames; excluded from the player application. It contributes no external content to the recording.

## Commercial status

The game uses original depictions of named real places. Original authorship of meshes does not settle building image/brand, promotion or other commercial rights. Sydney Opera House image/brand commercial clearance is unresolved. No third-party permission, legal clearance, purchase, store submission or approval has been claimed or executed. Airport buildings are original approximations and use no airline logos. The public preview permits personal play and testing under `../PLAY_PERMISSION.md`; the original source has not been assigned an open-source licence. Third-party components and data retain their own licences. Free publication does not establish third-party commercial clearance.
